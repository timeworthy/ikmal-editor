ikmal editor · locked asset set

Every asset, from two drawings.

Generated live from edmark.js — the locked two-tier mark in sage. Nothing here is hand-drawn, so a change in the export studio flows through every asset on this page. The full tier ("ikmal" in the ring) is used at 48px and up; the minimum tier (one line of prose, squiggle broken at 64 / 78%) takes over below that.

Every tile has its own download. SVG is the source of truth; PNG buttons rasterize at the size named on the tile.

Favicons
